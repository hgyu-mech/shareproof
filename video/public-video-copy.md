ShareProof suggests email and phone regions with local English OCR, supports manual covers, and inspects those covers in the final exported PNG. This 2:20 demo shows actual browser interaction states captured and edited into a silent sequence. The receipt is an original synthetic sample.

The check applies to selected regions. OCR can miss details; manual review remains necessary. It does not certify complete anonymization.
